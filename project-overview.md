# Project Overview

## Purpose

Design and development of a custom magnetron metal sputtering reactor for vacuum deposition research.

## Engineering Workstreams

- Electromagnetic modelling using FEMM and ANSYS Maxwell
- Magnetic-field extraction, processing and validation
- Magnetron mechanical design and material selection
- Target cooling and thermal management
- Vacuum system and sealing interfaces
- Plasma simulation and future deposition modelling
- Experimental validation

## Current Research Priorities

1. Preserve and document original magnetic simulation exports.
2. Verify coordinate conventions and field-component definitions.
3. Automate magnetic-field data processing and unit checks.
4. Compare FEMM and ANSYS Maxwell under equivalent conditions.
5. Progress toward plasma modelling using JC-PIC.

## Evidence Policy

Distinguish simulated results, analytical calculations, engineering hypotheses and experimentally validated findings. Record assumptions and limitations alongside each result.
